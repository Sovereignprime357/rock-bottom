## What changed

Nine original 128px procedural tiles distinguish masonry, plaster, road, concrete, wood, worn
metal, cloth, skin and rubber. They share cached mipmapped textures. Storefronts have more
structure; Pete has shelves, merchandise, a register and visible interior fixtures. The streets
and yard gain an original battered sedan, cabinets, mailbox, stoop, workbench and tire stacks.
Sky/fog follow day and night. Standing additions have collision and clear interaction routes.

The existing cast has fitted facial planes, layered clothes/seams, better shoes, hands and
creature details. Tony keeps three coats and never blinks; Pete's snack follows his arm; Brutus
and the possum have bounded gestures. Fists are slimmer and no longer cast detached world shadows.
HUD corner plates reveal more street. Lurch's hit reaction now freezes with paused gameplay.

Static mesh leaves merge by compatible render state and spatial region. Kit palette tints become
sRGB vertex colors, preserving the rendered palette while sharing surfaces. World construction
reduces 1,266 static mesh leaves to 142 batches. Collision, labels, fixtures, target handles and
animation pivots stay independent. No per-frame meshes or texture generation were introduced.
